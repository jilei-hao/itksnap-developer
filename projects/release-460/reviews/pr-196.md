## Quality of the 33 changes

They are careful corrections, in the terms Chinese imaging software normally uses. This is my
reading; a native speaker on the team could confirm it.

| Kind | Examples |
|---|---|
| Wrong word fixed | "Build" 版本 (version) → 构建; "Filter:" 滤镜 (photo filter) → 过滤 |
| Standard imaging terms | intensity 强度 → 灰度; volume rendering 体积渲染/体渲染 → 体绘制; subsample 子采样 → 降采样; annotation 注释 → 标注 |
| Color maps | Hot, Cool, Copper and the four seasons are kept in English as proper names. "Black to red" etc. gain 渐变 (gradient) |
| Tidier wording | 通过 SSH 隧道连接, 最近图像, 文件头大小, 将相机对准 |

**Small leftovers, not blocking:** the same terms still use the old wording in four tooltips:
- two for volume rendering: `LayerInspectorRowDelegate` "Enable volume rendering of this layer", and
  one `ColorMapInspector` tooltip;
- two for annotation: the Annotation Mode and Annotation Tool Inspector tooltips.

The one remaining 强度 is right: it translates "edge strength", not "intensity".
